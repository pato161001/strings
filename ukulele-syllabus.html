Courses / Ukulele

Ukulele syllabus

Three levels that take you from your first strum to fingerpicking, chord melody and arranging your own songs. Quick to start, great for kids and adults alike.

Levels
Beginner, intermediate, advanced
Classes
40 minutes, 4 or 8 a month
Where
3 studios or online
Exams
ROCKSCHOOL ukulele grades

Your path

Your first class works out where you fit, so you can start at the right level. Everyone moves through it at their own pace.

01

Level 1 · Beginner

First strums

For complete beginners of any age. You will be playing songs within the first few classes.

  • Holding the ukulele, the strumming hand and how to practise
  • Tuning (G C E A), reading chord charts and tabs
  • First chords: C, Am, F, G, G7, Em and D
  • Strumming patterns: down-up, the island strum and 3/4 time
  • Changing chords in time with a metronome
  • Simple melodies on the A and E strings

By the endStrum and sing along to your favourite songs

02

Level 2 · Intermediate

Beyond strumming

For players who are comfortable with the basic chords and want more colour and control.

  • Barre shapes (Bb, Bm), 7th, minor 7th and sus chords
  • Moving chord shapes up the neck
  • Fingerpicking patterns and arpeggios
  • Chucks, triplet strums and other percussive techniques
  • The major scale, keys and common progressions
  • Ear training: hearing chord changes and working out songs by ear

By the endPlay songs in any common key, picked or strummed

03

Level 3 · Advanced

Make it your own

For confident players who want to arrange, solo and perform.

  • Chord melody arrangements of songs you love
  • Campanella style and low-G tuning
  • Improvising with major and minor pentatonic scales
  • Swing, reggae and calypso rhythms and syncopation
  • Arranging your own songs and playing with others
  • Performance practice

By the endArrange pieces, solo and perform with confidence

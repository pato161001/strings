Meet your instructor

Deepak Gochhayat

Founder, music educator, guitar and ukulele instructor

Hello! I'm Deepak Gochhayat, a musician, passionate music educator and the founder of The Strings Theory.

My journey into music has been driven by a deep passion for playing, learning and teaching. Although I hold a B.Tech degree in Information Technology, my love for music inspired me to pursue a career in music education and build an institute where aspiring musicians could learn with the right guidance and structure.

Guitar and ukulele

Over the years, I have specialised in teaching Guitar and Ukulele, particularly to beginners and intermediate learners.

My teaching approach focuses on building strong fundamentals, developing practical musical skills and helping students become independent and confident musicians.

My teaching philosophy

I believe learning an instrument should be an enjoyable and rewarding experience, not an overwhelming one.

My teaching methodology combines:

  • Practical instrument training
  • Music theory
  • Rhythm development
  • Ear training
  • Structured exercises

I focus on understanding each student's learning pace and creating a personalised learning experience.

Alongside my own structured curriculum, I guide students preparing for ROCKSCHOOL and Trinity College London graded examinations.

Teaching students across the world

Through The Strings Theory, I have been able to connect with students from different parts of the world. I particularly enjoy working with NRI students, international learners and working professionals who want to pursue music alongside their busy schedules.

My goal is to make quality music education accessible regardless of geographical boundaries.

  • NRI students
  • International learners
  • Working professionals
  • Beginners and intermediates

Why I started The Strings Theory

I founded The Strings Theory with a simple vision: to make learning Guitar and Ukulele accessible, structured and enjoyable for everyone.

What started as a passion for teaching has grown into a music education platform offering online and offline learning opportunities.

For me, teaching music is not just about helping someone play songs. It's about building confidence, creativity, discipline and a lifelong connection with music.

I look forward to being a part of your musical journey!

Deepak
